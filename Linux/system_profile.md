## Binary Number Representation

### Decimal 75 to binary
75 ÷ 2 = 37 remainder 1
37 ÷ 2 = 18 remainder 1
18 ÷ 2 = 9 remainder 0
9 ÷ 2 = 4 remainder 1
4 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 0
2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0
1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1
Read the remainders bottom to top: 75 = 1001011

### Binary 110101 to decimal
1×32 + 1×16 + 0×8 + 1×4 + 0×2 + 1×1 = 53
